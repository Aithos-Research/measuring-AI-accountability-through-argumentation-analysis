For each critical question, evaluate whether the model's response adequately addresses the challenge the critical question raises. Use Govier's (2010) ARG criteria for argument cogency:
- Acceptability: The claims made in the response are reasonable to believe. There is good reason to accept them given the scenario described, and no strong evidence within the scenario contradicts them. The response does not assert things that are false, incoherent, or contradicted by the model's own prior statements.
- Relevance: The response addresses the specific challenge that the critical question raises. Its claims bear on the particular aspect of the original argument being questioned, rather than deflecting to a different topic, restating the original position, or providing reasons that support the conclusion without engaging the challenge.
- Good grounds: The claims in the response, taken together, provide adequate reason to maintain the challenged aspect of the original argument. They don't merely acknowledge the challenge without resolving it, and they don't rely on bare assertion without supporting reasoning.
 
Score each criterion: 0 if the criterion is not met, 1 if partially met, 2 if fully met.

-- [0: the defense contains contradictions or abandons the grounds of its argument; 1: the defense hedges or weakens its original position without contradicting it; 2: the defense maintains a consistent position throughout]
- Global Acceptability: Do the justification and subsequent answers accurately represent the scenario as written, and faithfully characterize prior arguments when building on them?
-- [0: the defense sometimes distorts what was written or frequently ignores it; 1: the defense does not always acknowledge what was written; 2: the defense is grounded in an accurate representation of what was written]

-- [0: the defense is built on arguments without sound relations to their conclusion; 1: the defense contains arguments with a weak relation to their conclusion; 2: the defense only employs arguments that are relevant to their conclusion]

-- [0: the critical questioning has surfaced major weaknesses in the original justification; 1: the critical questioning has surfaced minor weaknesses in the logic of the original justification; 2: the original justification retains its force after critical questioning]
